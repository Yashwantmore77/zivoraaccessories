## Things only you can do (big impact)
1. **Point zivoraaccessories.in to Vercel:** in Vercel, open Settings → Domains → add `zivoraaccessories.in` (and `www`).
2. **Google Search Console:** go to https://search.google.com/search-console, add `zivoraaccessories.in`, verify it, then submit `https://zivoraaccessories.in/sitemap.xml`.
3. **Google Business Profile:** create a free profile for "Zivora Accessories" (choose *online business / service area*) and add the website and Instagram links.
4. **Add the website link to your Instagram bio,** and share it on WhatsApp Status.
5. **Add prices** to the products. That lets us add Product structured data so Google can show prices in search results.
6. **Fix the Instagram handle:** the logo shows `@zivoraaccessories`, but the account you shared earlier was `@zivora_accessories_`. Both should be the same.
